:::ingredients
### Soup
- 2 tablespoons light olive oil
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 1-2 jalapeños, seeded and diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 rotisserie chicken
- 32 oz chicken broth
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 1/2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- 1/3 cup cilantro, chopped, plus more to garnish
- 1 1/2 tablespoons lime juice (from 1 lime), or to taste

### Tortilla Strips
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 8 corn tortillas (6 inch)

### Toppings
- 1 large avocado, diced
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Sour cream
:::

:::instructions
### Tortilla Strips
1. Heat the **olive oil** in a pan over medium-high heat. Cut the **tortillas** into thin strips and fry in batches until crisp. Drain on a paper towel and set aside, adding more **oil** between batches as needed.

### Soup
1. Heat the **olive oil** in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the **onion** and **jalapeños** and sauté until softened. Add the **garlic** and stir for 30-60 seconds, until fragrant.
2. Break down the **rotisserie chicken**: pull the meat off the bones, discard the skin and bones, and shred the meat.
3. Add the **chicken broth**, **crushed tomatoes**, **corn**, **black beans**, **chili powder**, **cumin**, and **salt** to the pot. Bring to a boil, then simmer for about 20 minutes.
4. Add the shredded **chicken** to the soup and simmer another 5 minutes. Remove from heat and stir in the **cilantro** and **lime juice**.
5. Serve garnished with **tortilla strips**, **avocado**, **sour cream**, fresh **cilantro**, and **lime wedges**.
:::

:::notes
### Storage
- Refrigerate for 3-4 days or freeze for up to 2 months. Store without toppings.
